How this works

Each card below is a community signing certificate. Tapping Install installs MuffinEMU straight onto your device over the air — no computer and no AltStore. Apple revokes these certificates from time to time, which is why several are listed: if one stops opening, come back and install with another. Your games and saves are kept.

This is an unaffiliated, non-commercial installer built on the open-source SideInstaller framework.

Trust the certificate

A one-time step so iOS will open the app.

  1. When Safari asks, tap Install / Allow, then wait for MuffinEMU to appear on your Home Screen.
  2. Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  3. Tap the developer profile under Enterprise App, then tap Trust.
  4. Launch MuffinEMU. If it won't open or says the certificate is revoked, come back and install with a different one.
3

Turn on JIT & add your keys

Needed for full speed and for encrypted retail games.

Installing this way re-signs the app, which strips the JIT entitlement — the same as SideStore. So JIT is attached separately:

  1. Attach a JIT enabler — StikJIT, or the JIT attach in SideStore or LiveContainer.
  2. Turn the recompiler on in Settings → CPU inside MuffinEMU.
  3. Add keys.txt from your own Wii U for encrypted retail games — drop it into the keys folder in the Files app, or import it in Settings. Homebrew needs no keys.
Full instructions. JIT, keys.txt and encrypted game folders are covered in detail in the official installation guide.
